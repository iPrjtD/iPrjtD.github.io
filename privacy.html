Privacy policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

yt-agent ("the app") is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer and is used only with the owner's own Google account and YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

With the account owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app may access:

  • Basic channel information (channel ID, title, playlists).
  • The channel's own videos and their metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy status).
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, retention, subscribers gained).

The app also reads publicly available trend data (for example news feeds and public trending lists). It does not access data about any other YouTube user.

How the data is used

  • To upload videos to the owner's channel as private drafts, update their metadata, add them to playlists and schedule or publish them after the owner approves.
  • To measure how the owner's videos perform and improve future videos.

The data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used for any purpose other than operating the owner's own channel.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens and analytics data are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local database and local files that are not shared or uploaded to any third-party server. Communication with Google uses HTTPS.

Sharing

The app does not share Google or YouTube data with anyone. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube itself, as needed to perform the actions above.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to operate the channel. The owner can delete the local data at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account connections page; after revocation the app can no longer access the account, and stored tokens become invalid.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
